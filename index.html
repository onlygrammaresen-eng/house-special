          How it works

          A house special is five layers

          1. Base — drip, cold brew, two shots, or matcha.
          2. Syrup — about 1 oz per 8–12 oz of drink.
          3. Milk — splash in cold brew, more in a latte.
          4. Foam — optional. Cold foam sits on iced drinks.
          5. Topping — cinnamon is enough most mornings.

          Homemade syrups are perishable. Keep them refrigerated and discard if they smell off. This page is a kitchen companion, not nutrition or medical advice.
